About Project Elevate Foundation

Elevating, Empowering and Transforming Lives

Our Story

Our Identity & Purpose

Project Elevate Foundation was founded by Dzifa Asomany Esq from a profound dedication to community development in Ghana, compassionate social support, and transforming lives. We are driven by the conviction that every person, regardless of their background or socioeconomic status, deserves access to quality education, practical skills, and opportunities for personal and economic growth.

Across underserved communities in Ghana, many children, young people, and vulnerable households face educational gaps and economic barriers. Project Elevate Foundation exists to bridge these gaps through structured mentorship, educational assistance, vocational training, and grassroots social support initiatives that cultivate self-reliance.

Working alongside passionate volunteers, community advocates, donors, and organizational partners, we foster community empowerment and meaningful social impact. Our initiatives are designed to create lasting solutions that uplift individuals and strengthen communities from within.

Dzifa Asomany Esq
Founder & Executive Director
Foundation Purpose

Vision, Mission & Values

🔭

Vision Statement

To create an empowered and self-reliant society where every individual, regardless of their background or circumstances, has access to quality education, opportunities for personal development and the skills needed to achieve a dignified and sustainable livelihood.

🎯

Mission Statement

To bridge educational gaps, promote access to quality learning opportunities and empower individuals, particularly young people and underserved communities, through education, mentorship, skills acquisition and capacity-building initiatives that enhance self-reliance, economic independence and sustainable development.

💎

Core Values

  • ✦ Educational Equity & Access
  • ✦ Youth Mentorship & Guidance
  • ✦ Dignity, Capacity & Self-Reliance
  • ✦ Sustainable Economic Independence
Who We Serve

Target Beneficiaries

Project Elevate Foundation primarily focuses on individuals and communities that face educational, social, and economic barriers.

🧒

Underprivileged Children

Children from disadvantaged backgrounds who lack access to adequate educational resources, learning materials, and schooling opportunities.

🏫

Schools & Educational Institutions

Particularly underserved schools that require support in improving teaching and learning environments, educational resources, and academic standards.

🌟

Adolescents & Young People

Young people who require guidance, mentorship, career direction, personal development, and opportunities to discover and maximize their potential.

👩

Women & Girls

Individuals who need empowerment through education, mentorship, vocational training, entrepreneurship, and personal development opportunities.

🛠️

Unemployed & Underemployed Individuals

Persons who require practical skills, vocational training, and capacity development to improve their employability and economic independence.

🏡

Vulnerable Households & Communities

Communities requiring interventions that promote education, skills development, economic empowerment, and improved living conditions.

How We Deliver Impact

5 Strategic Focus Areas

Organizing the Foundation's activities around five key pillars for sustainable and transformative impact.

Pillar 01

Educational Development

Bridging educational gaps through school support, learning materials, infrastructure assistance, and initiatives that improve the quality of education.

Pillar 02

Youth Empowerment & Mentorship

Equipping young people with confidence, leadership skills, career guidance, and the knowledge to make informed life decisions.

Pillar 03

Skills Acquisition & Vocational Training

Providing practical and market-relevant skills that promote employability, entrepreneurship, and financial independence.

Pillar 04

Livelihood & Economic Empowerment

Supporting individuals to develop sustainable income-generating activities and improve their economic circumstances.

Pillar 05

Community Development

Identifying community needs and implementing interventions that promote social welfare, inclusion, and sustainable development.

Our Journey

1 Year of Impact (2026)

A month-by-month journey of transformative milestones achieved from January to October 2026.

January

Foundation Established

Dzifa Asomany Esq formally incorporates Project Elevate Foundation in Accra, Ghana, assembling an initial team of dedicated community leaders and volunteers.

February

Grassroots Community Assessment

Field teams completed exhaustive needs assessments across rural districts and peri-urban centers, prioritizing schools and healthcare clinics for intervention.

March

Elevate Learn Initiative

Launched our flagship education programme, distributing textbooks, backpacks, and essential STEM learning kits to over 1,200 primary school pupils.

April

Clean Water & Sanitation Drive

Commissioned solar-powered boreholes and hygienic wash stations, delivering safe drinking water to over 2,000 community residents.

May

Elevate Her Women's Accelerator

Graduated an inaugural cohort of 150 women entrepreneurs with certified business training, mentor pairing, and seed microgrants.

June

Youth Tech & Digital Hub

Opened our first community digital centre equipped with modern computers and high-speed internet, offering free coding and digital literacy workshops.

July

Mid-Year Health Outreach

Hosted multi-day mobile health clinics providing free eye exams, maternal health consultations, and essential medication to 3,500+ attendees.

August

Back-to-School Scholarship Awards

Awarded comprehensive secondary school scholarships, uniforms, and mentorship to 250 high-achieving students from vulnerable households.

September

Green Future Climate Action

Mobilized 800+ youth volunteers across Ghana to plant 12,000 indigenous trees and establish sustainable school vegetable gardens.

October

10-Month Impact Milestone

Surpassed 10,000 direct beneficiaries across our educational, medical, and community programmes, establishing strong regional partnerships.
