Our Story

How It All Began

Project Elevate Foundation was founded in 2026 from a simple but powerful idea: that every person, regardless of where they're born, deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential.

Our founder, Dzifa Asomany Esq, witnessed first-hand the devastating effects of inequality during her years as a field physician in East Africa. Fuelled by this experience, she rallied a team of educators, engineers, and community leaders to build something lasting.

Today, we operate across 12 countries with over 2,000 volunteers, 120+ active programmes, and a community of more than 50,000 lives directly touched by our work.

Our Journey

1 Year of Impact (2026)

A month-by-month journey of transformative milestones achieved from January to October 2026.

January

Foundation Established

Dzifa Asomany Esq formally incorporates Project Elevate Foundation in Accra, Ghana, assembling an initial team of dedicated community leaders and volunteers.

February

Grassroots Community Assessment

Field teams completed exhaustive needs assessments across rural districts and peri-urban centers, prioritizing schools and healthcare clinics for intervention.

March

Elevate Learn Initiative

Launched our flagship education programme, distributing textbooks, backpacks, and essential STEM learning kits to over 1,200 primary school pupils.

April

Clean Water & Sanitation Drive

Commissioned solar-powered boreholes and hygienic wash stations, delivering safe drinking water to over 2,000 community residents.

May

Elevate Her Women's Accelerator

Graduated an inaugural cohort of 150 women entrepreneurs with certified business training, mentor pairing, and seed microgrants.

June

Youth Tech & Digital Hub

Opened our first community digital centre equipped with modern computers and high-speed internet, offering free coding and digital literacy workshops.

July

Mid-Year Health Outreach

Hosted multi-day mobile health clinics providing free eye exams, maternal health consultations, and essential medication to 3,500+ attendees.

August

Back-to-School Scholarship Awards

Awarded comprehensive secondary school scholarships, uniforms, and mentorship to 250 high-achieving students from vulnerable households.

September

Green Future Climate Action

Mobilized 800+ youth volunteers across Ghana to plant 12,000 indigenous trees and establish sustainable school vegetable gardens.

October

10-Month Impact Milestone

Surpassed 10,000 direct beneficiaries across our educational, medical, and community programmes, establishing strong regional partnerships.
